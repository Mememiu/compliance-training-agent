# Learning Score Direction Contract

THESIS: Training is a readable score: the product makes the next learning action visible and makes progress feel formed, not merely checked. It refuses the generic blue dashboard and the card wall as the primary identity.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Paper Ground, deep Score Ink, quiet Steel Note annotations, stepped notation blocks, aligned staff lines, margin notes, and one rare Signal Lime current-step marker. Surfaces are flat and paper-like; hierarchy comes from ink, spacing, and meaningful marks.

STORY: An employee arrives, sees the next lesson in the learning path, understands why it matters, and continues with confidence. A course owner sees the same structure as editable modules, version notes, and dependable status.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The employee home leads with one visible learning path and one Signal Lime next action. The current course and lesson sit on a calm paper reading field; the catalog and secondary progress details follow below or to the side. The independent mark anchors the rail, while the replaceable product name remains a wordmark layer.

FORM: Learning Score / Labanotation-inspired notation, assigned in the direction seed as c1478ce0. The Acetate Manual challenger donated scannable module segmentation; the Darkroom challenger donated a continuous before/after completion reveal. These are system disciplines, not decorative skins.
